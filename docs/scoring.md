# Scoring Methodology (Phase 1 baseline)

Initial weighted hypothesis:

- Activity: 20%
- Consistency: 20%
- Contribution Depth: 25%
- Project Quality: 20%
- Open Source: 15%

Weights are centralized in `lib/scoring/weights.ts` and validated by unit tests.

Scores are deterministic, explainable signals and not absolute judgments of ability.
